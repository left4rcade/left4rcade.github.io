Community guidelines

Requests and notes are public. Please submit genuine game suggestions and use appropriate language. Offensive content, spam, duplicate requests, or vote manipulation may be removed. Do not include personal or sensitive information. A hashed identifier derived from your network address and browser information may be used to prevent duplicate votes and limit abuse. The application does not store raw IP addresses in its game-request or poll tables; provider-level logs may be handled separately.
